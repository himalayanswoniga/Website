Our Story

From Nepal's Highlands
to Your Kitchen

Himalayan Swoniga Harvest was born from a deep respect for Nepal's centuries-old farming traditions. We source garlic, ginger, lapsi, and other highland crops directly from trusted local farmers — and process them using both natural sun-drying and modern machine drying to guarantee consistent quality year-round.

  • Direct partnerships with highland farmers across Nepal
  • Dual-process drying — sun-dried and machine-dried for year-round consistency
  • Fine-milled to preserve full flavour, aroma, and nutrition
  • Zero artificial additives, colours, or preservatives
